How to play

  1. Read the order above each customer (also listed on the right).
  2. Drag and drop everything with the mouse. The ring turns green where you can drop and red where you can't.
  3. Drag ingredients out of the crates on the back counter.
  4. Cook: drag patties/pork onto the grill, noodles into the pot, potatoes into the fryer. Drag them off when they're done, before they burn!
  5. Click a dispenser to pour a drink or broth, then drag it to the board when it's full.
  6. Drop each finished part on the assembly board, then drag the whole plate to the customer.
  7. Faster service earns bigger tips and combos.
  8. Hit the earnings target before the shift ends, and don't let too many customers walk out.
