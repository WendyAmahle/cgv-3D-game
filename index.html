How to play

  1. Read the order above each customer (also listed on the right).
  2. Grab ingredients from the crates on the back counter.
  3. Cook: drop patties/pork on the grill, noodles in the pot, potatoes in the fryer. Take them off when they're done, before they burn!
  4. Click a dispenser to pour a drink or broth, then grab it when it's full.
  5. Drop each finished part on the assembly board, then pick up the whole plate.
  6. Click the customer to serve. Faster service earns bigger tips and combos.
  7. Hit the earnings target before the shift ends, and don't let too many customers walk out.
