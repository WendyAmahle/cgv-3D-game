## Original work

- Procedural food, stations, serving counters, noodle-bar structures, and diner buildings. The scanned burger bun is sliced and assembled in code.
- Animation-retargeting code that applies X Bot animations to the other skeletons.
- Gameplay systems, recipes, customer and scoring logic, item physics, UI, level configurations, audio mixing, seamless looping, and 3D sound placement.
- Runtime Web Audio API synthesis for Level 1 music and the level-complete, level-failed, and trophy jingles.
- Custom GLSL shaders for cooking, liquid fill, molecular cocktail mixing, holographic orders, steam, headlight beams, boiling water, noodle-pot steam plume, particles, sky, colour grading, and heat haze.
